Capabilities

Everything you need in one tool

Independent conversion modes, batch processing, and live map visualization — built for Lagos Cadastral Survey data.

LCS 165P → NTM

Convert Lagos Cadastral Surveys (LCS 165P) offsets, in feet, straight to Nigeria Transverse Mercator coordinates — no UTM zone required.

NTM → UTM

Transform NTM belt coordinates directly into UTM, choosing between Minna datum or WGS84 — independent of any LCS 165P input.

LCS 165P → UTM

Transform LCS 165P directly into UTM — independent of any NTM input.

Live Map Plotting

Every converted point — single or batch — is plotted instantly on an interactive Leaflet map so you can visually verify your survey data.

Batch CSV Processing

Upload hundreds of coordinate pairs at once. Get converted NTM, UTM, and lat/lon values back in a downloadable CSV file.

Unit Converter

Quick conversions between feet, metres, acres, hectares, and square yards — common survey units, always within reach.

Process

How the conversion works

From a historical Lagos survey offset to a pin on a modern map — in three simple steps.

01

Enter LCS 165P Coordinates

Input your LCS 165P East/West and North/South offsets in feet, exactly as recorded in the Lagos Cadastral Survey.

02

NTM Conversion

The app converts your offsets into NTM belt coordinates, in metres, using a proprietary tie-point reference.

03

UTM Transformation

NTM coordinates are projected into UTM Zone 31N/32N/33N, in either Minna datum or WGS84.

04

Visualize on Map

The final WGS84 latitude/longitude is plotted instantly as a pin on the interactive map.
