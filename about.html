About the LCS 165P COORDINATE CONVERTER

A tool built to make Lagos Cadastral Survey (LCS 165P) data instantly usable in modern geospatial workflows — converting between LCS 165P, NTM, and UTM with map visualization and batch processing.

Project Overview

Why this project exists

Many cadastral records across Lagos still exist in the LCS 165P system — a local reference grid that predates the wider adoption of the Nigeria Transverse Mercator (NTM) and Universal Transverse Mercator (UTM) systems used in modern GIS and mapping work today. Moving between these systems by hand is slow and error-prone, especially when dealing with large datasets of survey points.

This project was built to close that gap — giving surveyors, students, and GIS practitioners a fast, reliable way to convert LCS 165P coordinates into NTM and UTM, visualize the results on an interactive map, and process entire batches of points at once, without needing specialised desktop software.

Developed as a B.Sc. final year project in the Department of Surveying & Geoinformatics, University of Lagos, the application focuses on accuracy, usability, and making legacy survey data accessible in a modern, web-based format.
